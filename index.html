DownTop

Genre
2D top-down shooter
Status
Finished in September 2026
Engine
Unity
Language
C#

Built with

  • Unity
  • C#
  • GitHub

Features

  • Turrets that you have to destroy by shooting them
  • Keys that open locked doors
  • Walls you break to get further into the building

Challenges I overcame

  • The hardest part was making sure the player and the turrets didn't hit themselves with their own bullets. A bullet was spawned inside the shooter, so it hit its own shooter straight away.

    I gave the player and every turret a spawn point just in front of them, outside their own collider. Bullets now appear there, so they never touch the one who fired them.

What I'd do differently next time

Test shooting with the player and the turrets much earlier, so problems like this show up straight away instead of late in the project.

Dungeon Crawler

Genre
2D round-based dungeon crawler
Status
Finished in May 2026
Engine
Unity
Language
C#

Built with

  • Unity
  • C#
  • GitHub

Features

  • Enemies get stronger every round
  • Earn coins by defeating enemies
  • A shop appears after every round, where you choose which shop you want to buy from
  • Upgrade shop: extra hearts and a shield
  • Weapon shop: an iron sword and a battle axe

Challenges I overcame

  • The hardest part was making the shop appear only when a round was really finished, and not while enemies were still alive.

    I wrote a script that counts how many enemies are still alive. Every time an enemy is defeated the number goes down, and when it reaches zero the round is over and the shop spawns.

What I'd do differently next time

Plan out all the systems first, like rounds, coins and the shops, before I start building, so I know how they fit together.

Dev log

Short posts about what I'm learning and building.

28 September 2026

Making Cozy Ninja feel cozy

Fruit Ninja is fast and loud. I wanted the same fun, but calm and wintery. Here's what I changed.

Read the full post

Instead of fruit, the player slices Christmas food. The presents work like the bombs in Fruit Ninja: slice one and you lose a heart.

Warm colours and soft sounds do most of the work. The rules barely changed, but the game feels completely different.

29 May 2026

Balancing rounds in my dungeon crawler

If enemies get stronger every round, the player needs to get stronger too. The shop is where that happens.

Read the full post

After every round a shop appears. The player chooses between a better weapon, a shield or buying health back. That choice is the heart of the game.

If items are too cheap the game gets boring, and if they are too expensive it gets frustrating. Playtesting was the only way to find the right prices.
